Enthusiast-engineered architecture

MOTO LOGG was created out of personal frustration with generic expense managers. When riding a motorcycle across thousands of kilometers, riders do not have the time or focus to pull over, unlock an app, enter ₹350, type in "HPCL Fuel Pump", choose a category, and save the receipt.

Instead, MOTO LOGG uses a native Android Kotlin BroadcastReceiver that listens strictly to system SMS delivery events. The moment a bank alert arrives from SBI, HDFC, ICICI, or Axis, the TransactionParser extracts the transaction debit, merchant entity, and category using high-speed regex routines.

The deduplication shield

Because Android may trigger multiple broadcast notifications for multi-part SMS messages, a DuplicateManager generates a SHA-256 fingerprint from the combined merchant, amount, and timestamp, guaranteeing that no transaction is ever recorded twice. A battery-optimized actionable system notification is raised, enabling riders to confirm and categorize expenses with a single tap directly from their lock screen.

Frequently asked questions

How do I install the downloaded APK on my Android phone?

1. Tap the Download APK button above to download motologg.apk.
2. Open your downloaded file notification or locate it in your device's Files / Downloads folder.
3. When Android displays "Install unknown apps" security warning, tap Settings and switch on "Allow from this source".
4. Tap Install. MOTO LOGG requests SMS permissions strictly to process bank debit SMS notifications locally on your device.

Does MOTO LOGG upload my private SMS messages?

Never. The Android SMS parser runs strictly locally on your physical device. Only the finalized transaction debit details that you explicitly confirm are synced to your private Cloud Firestore collection.

What motorcycles are supported?

The virtual garage includes preconfigured profiles with OEM tank capacities and reserve thresholds for models like the Bajaj Pulsar NS200, Honda Unicorn 160, Yamaha MT-15, KTM Duke, Royal Enfield Classic 350, alongside custom displacement bikes.

How is fuel economy calculated?

MOTO LOGG calculates fuel economy (km/L) and running cost per kilometer (₹/km) using the full-to-full tank odometer delta method, accounting for reserve fuel margins.
