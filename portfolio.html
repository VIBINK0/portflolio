THE PORTFOLIO

A point of view.
In every build.

Production systems, real-time IoT telemetry pipelines, enterprise monitors, and motorcycle software engineered by Vibin K.

One engineering mind, eight commercial applications

This portfolio covers commercial software engineered across two demanding environments: distributed real-time industrial IoT platforms at Mobitech Wireless Solutions, and user-facing mobile apps, offline synchronization engines, and telemetry platforms at Lawpoc Systems — alongside my independent motorcycle telemetry product, MOTO LOGG.

Bridging these worlds is where the real engineering value lies: linking edge IoT nodes over low-latency WebSockets and MQTT with microsecond command accuracy, while architecting intuitive, responsive Flutter frontends that keep users in complete control without battery drain or latency hitches.

Industrial IoT & smart agriculture: Mobitech Wireless Solutions

At Mobitech Wireless Solutions, I spearheaded the frontend architecture for agricultural telemetry platforms deployed to thousands of farmers and industrial pump stations:

  • DCON AG: Flagship smart agriculture automation platform connecting remote IoT nodes via WebSockets and MQTT with microsecond schedule triggers and sensor monitoring.
  • Flocon (OMS): Centralized operations monitoring system for reservoir-to-field flow automation, dynamic canal pressure alerts, and canvas-rendered 60fps telemetry charts.
  • TCON: Resilient, low-bandwidth timer starter controller engineered for small-to-medium farming motors with automated 3-phase voltage and dry-run safety protections.

Cross-platform mobile applications: Lawpoc Systems

At Lawpoc Systems, I engineered and published a diverse suite of production mobile applications across navigation, video analytics, on-demand logistics, and enterprise operations:

  • Ride N Repeat: Dedicated navigation and convoy tracking companion for motorcycle clubs featuring live group GPS pings, route deviation warnings, and automated crash/SOS emergency detection.
  • ALA (User App): Fast hyperlocal on-demand food and grocery delivery mobile app with live courier polyline routing, push status notifications, and instant cart checkout.
  • Wisually: 1-to-1 video feedback and customer experience platform with custom camera/audio recorders, chunked media uploads, and sentiment analytics.
  • Trackki: Scalable enterprise management portal featuring dynamic offline-first form builders, automatic background reconnection queues, and asset tracking.
  • Majic Astros: Real-time astrology consultation application integrating Firebase Realtime Database status streams and multi-channel payment reconciliation (Razorpay, PhonePe, Cashfree, Stripe).

The flagship suite: MOTO LOGG

MOTO LOGG is my independent flagship product: an enthusiast-engineered Flutter application designed for motorcycle riders who want to track every penny spent on their machines. Built with Riverpod v3, Cloud Firestore, and a native Android Kotlin BroadcastReceiver (SmsReceiver.kt), it automatically detects debit SMS alerts from Indian banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis) and parses merchant details, amounts, and fuel categories with a SHA-256 deduplication shield.
